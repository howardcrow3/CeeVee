# Changelog

User-facing notes, one section per release. The release workflow copies the section whose heading matches the tag (for example `## 1.0.0`).

## Unreleased

- A clearer Sign in with Google button, shown in setup and in Settings under Accounts. It is optional, says it is read-only, and shows a waiting state with Cancel.
- Applications is a board with a column for each stage (Draft, Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer, Closed), the top five cards in each and "+N more". A Table view is one click away, and items waiting on you sit in a strip at the top.
- Jobs is a compact list: one line per job, filters for New and Saved plus a text filter, and a detail panel on the right with the actions for the job you click.
- Network: a people list you can search and sort into close, warm and weak, with contact import from vCard and CSV files (Google, Outlook, phone).
- Job types in the sidebar: each job type is a saved search you can turn on or off with a switch. They replace Kinds of jobs in Settings.
- Settings is now a window with tabs down the left (General, Job search, Accounts, Privacy and about), opened from the account menu.
- A tighter sidebar with an account menu at the bottom left: Settings, Send feedback and a System, Light or Dark theme choice.
- New look: Inter throughout, smaller and tighter controls, rounded corners, and matching light and dark themes.
- First internal builds.
- A 14-day trial, license key entry and a Buy button. After the trial CeeVee is read-only until a license is activated.
- Email tracking: connect Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud or any mailbox with an app password. CeeVee reads replies from employers, links them to your applications, updates their status and drafts a reply for you to edit. The new Inbox screen shows it all.
- Optional Google sign-in for Gmail (read-only) where available.
- CeeVee now reads your mail for anything that looks like a job update or a recruiter message, whoever sends it. It starts tracking jobs it finds (for you to confirm or dismiss) and updates the ones you already have. Mail that is not about a job is not stored.

- CeeVee keeps a record of every form it fills in for you: the answers it used, the resume and cover letter it attached, and the job post as it was that day.
- Before you submit an application, CeeVee lists every field it filled, every required field it could not answer, and anything to double check, so nothing goes out blank.
- Your first search now works right away: CeeVee also looks at the job boards of about 30 well-known companies, so you see real openings before you add anything. You can turn this off.
- A new People list keeps the people you know: how you know them, how close you are, notes, and a timeline of every touch. You can bring people in from a LinkedIn export, a vCard file or a contacts CSV, and CeeVee merges duplicates and shows who changed jobs when you import a newer LinkedIn export.
