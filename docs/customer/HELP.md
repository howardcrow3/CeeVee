# CeeVee help

## Install

**Windows 11:** run `CeeVee-setup.exe`. It installs for your user account and needs no administrator password. If Windows SmartScreen warns you, choose "More info", then "Run anyway" only if you downloaded the file from turtlebox.studio.

**Mac (Apple silicon):** open `CeeVee.dmg` and drag CeeVee to Applications.

CeeVee updates itself. Settings, About CeeVee, "Check for updates" checks right away.

## First steps

1. Open CeeVee. Your 14-day trial starts automatically.
2. Settings, Local AI: download the AI model (about 2.5 GB, one time). It runs on your computer.
3. Welcome page: paste your resume. CeeVee shows what it found; tick what to keep.
4. Settings, Job categories: add the kinds of jobs you want. Settings, Companies to watch: add companies.
5. CeeVee searches in the background and shows new jobs under Jobs. It keeps running in the system tray (Windows) or menu bar (Mac) when you close the window. Quit from there.

## Buying and your license

- Buy from Settings, License, "Buy CeeVee". The price is $19.99, once. One license works on 2 computers.
- Your key arrives by email. Click the activation link in the email, or paste the key into Settings, License.
- Move to a new computer: Settings, License, "Remove from this computer", then activate on the new one. Or use "Manage my license".
- After the trial with no license, CeeVee turns read-only: you can still look at and export everything.

## Problems

- **The model will not download.** Check your connection and free disk space (about 3 GB), then try again. Downloads resume.
- **CeeVee closed unexpectedly.** On the next start it says so. Settings, About CeeVee, "Open log folder", and send the files to support.
- **No jobs appear.** Add companies in Settings and a job category, then wait for the next search.
- **The activation link does nothing.** Paste the key into Settings, License instead.
- **Your computer's clock is wrong.** CeeVee notices clock changes and may report the trial as over. Fix the clock.

## Where your data is

Windows: `%APPDATA%\studio.turtlebox.ceevee`. Mac: `~/Library/Application Support/studio.turtlebox.ceevee`. Back it up by copying that folder while CeeVee is closed. See the privacy notice for what is sent over the internet.
