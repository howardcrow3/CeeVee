# CeeVee privacy notice

Last updated 2026-11-22.

CeeVee runs on your computer. Your resume, profile, jobs, documents, mail and chats are stored on your computer and are not sent to us.

## What stays on your computer

- Your resume, profile, job list, applications, documents and notes (in CeeVee's data folder).
- The AI model and everything you ask it. It runs locally; nothing you write to it leaves your computer.
- Your LinkedIn connections file, if you import one.
- A small log file and crash reports, to help fix problems. They hold no resume, job or email text. They leave your computer only if you send them to support yourself.

## What CeeVee sends over the internet

| When | To | What |
|---|---|---|
| First start, and when the trial or a license is checked | turtlebox.studio | A random machine identifier (a one-way hash, not your name or computer name), the app version, and your license key when you activate or remove it. |
| Update checks | dl.turtlebox.studio | A request for the update file list. No account or personal data. |
| Downloading the AI model | Hugging Face (a model host) | A normal file download. They see your IP address, as any website would. |
| Searching for jobs | The job boards and company career pages you choose | The same requests your browser would make to read public job lists. |
| Applying for a job | The employer's application site, through Chrome or Edge on your computer | What you submit in the application, the same as if you typed it yourself. |
| Buying | Paddle (our payment provider) | Your payment details go to Paddle, not to CeeVee. |

CeeVee has no advertising trackers.

## Optional: anonymous usage counts

The first time you open CeeVee it asks whether you want to share anonymous usage counts. The answer is No unless you choose Share, and you can change it any time in Settings, Usage analytics. While it is off, nothing is sent.

If you choose Share, CeeVee sends counts to Google Analytics: the app version, your operating system, which features you use (for example "ran a job search"), and whether CeeVee closed unexpectedly. It never sends your resume, jobs, email, chats, file names or anything you type. It uses a random number made only for this purpose, not your name, email, license key or computer identifier. Turning sharing off deletes that number. Google receives your IP address as any website would, and handles the counts under its own terms.

## Optional: Google

If you sign in with Google, CeeVee reads your email so it can notice recruiter replies and keep your applications up to date. The mail is read and stored on your computer. You can sign out at any time in Settings. Google may show a notice that CeeVee is not yet verified while its review is in progress.

## Your choices

- Delete all your CeeVee data: quit CeeVee and delete its data folder (Windows: `%APPDATA%\studio.turtlebox.ceevee`, Mac: `~/Library/Application Support/studio.turtlebox.ceevee`).
- Free a license seat: Settings, License, "Remove from this computer", or turtlebox.studio/ceevee/license.
- Questions: use the support link on turtlebox.studio/ceevee.
